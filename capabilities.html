01 · Mobile robotics · AI

Hiwonder JetAcker GPT-based robots

The lab’s two JetAcker platforms provide a hands-on testbed for developing and evaluating AI-enabled mobile-robot behaviors. Their ROS-based workflow supports rapid prototyping from perception and mapping through navigation and human–robot interaction.

Research activities

  • Mobile-robot perception, mapping, and autonomous navigation
  • Language-model-assisted task interpretation and human–robot interaction
  • Testing decision-making and control methods on physical platforms

02 · Mobile robotics · Experimental systems

Yahboom robotics platforms

A second set of mobile robots extends the lab’s hardware portfolio and gives students additional platforms for implementing and comparing autonomy methods across different systems.

Research activities

  • Robot software integration and autonomy prototyping
  • Perception, localization, planning, and feedback control
  • Repeatable experiments that connect simulation and hardware
